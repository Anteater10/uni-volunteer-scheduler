# The SciTrek program

SciTrek is a UCSB outreach program that puts university scientists into K-12 classrooms as mentors.
The scheduler exists to staff those classroom visits with volunteers.

**What SciTrek teaches.** SciTrek runs phenomenon-based, NGSS-aligned science curricula, designed by
science education experts and grounded in educational, sociocultural, and cognitive science. Students
explore a shared phenomenon through hands-on activities and experiments rather than through
lectures.

**What a SciTrek module is.** A module is a **five-session science experience** for a class. Each day
takes students through a series of activities and experiments examining one particular phenomenon.
SciTrek serves **secondary-level students** — middle and high school.

**What SciTrek's core strength is:** meaningful student–mentor interaction. The mentoring
relationship is the point of the program, not a side effect of delivering content.

**What SciTrek offers volunteers:**

- Science education through community.
- Professional development opportunities.
- Hands-on STEM module development and training.
- Volunteer incentives.
- **Course credit or volunteer hours.**

**Mentor training** happens through Orientation and the SciTrek Discovery Class. Orientation is
required before mentoring a module — see the orientation document for how the requirement works in
the app.

**How a module maps onto the app.** One module delivered at one school in one week is an **event**.
Each of the five sessions is a **slot** inside that event. Volunteers sign up **session by session**
and may take as many sessions as they want — there's no requirement to commit to all five.
