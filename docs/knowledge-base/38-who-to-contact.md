# Who to contact

**For a volunteer, the SciTrek office is the contact for everything.** If a volunteer doesn't know who
to ask, the answer is the SciTrek office — there's no separate escalation path, no per-school contact,
and no on-call number for volunteers to use.

The one named exception: **for course credit, email Gulistan Tansik at gulistan@ucsb.edu.**

A volunteer does not need to contact anyone to arrange transportation — SciTrek provides it — and does
not need to contact anyone to cancel, because cancelling is done through the link in their signup
email.

Emails the app sends (signup confirmations, reminders, broadcasts) come from SciTrek's configured
sending address, and replies go there rather than to an individual organizer.

**When the app itself is broken, staff contact Andy, the project owner.** This is a different question
from a volunteer's — nobody at the SciTrek office can fix the scheduler. Raise it with Andy in the
daily sync for anything that can wait, and for something urgent outside sync hours email
**siddhantandy@gmail.com**.

**What counts as urgent, for staff reporting a problem with the app:** anything that looks like damaged
or missing data — rosters or signups that have gone wrong or disappeared — is urgent and should go out
of hours. A screen that looks wrong but where the underlying information is intact is normal priority
and can wait for the sync. The same guidance is on the in-app Help page.
