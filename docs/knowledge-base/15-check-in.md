# Check-in and attendance

**Check-in is per slot, not per event.** A volunteer who is doing three sessions checks in three
times, once for each session they attend. Checking in records that they arrived; it does not by
itself finish anything.

**The check-in window runs from 30 minutes before a slot starts to 30 minutes after.** Outside that
window check-in is refused, and the door page tells the volunteer when the shift opens or that it
has closed rather than showing a locked screen with no explanation. The window opens half an hour
early because volunteers reliably arrive early.

There are two ways a volunteer gets checked in.

**The organizer taps them in on the roster.** This is the normal path. Open the event from
Operations, find the row, tap. It's safe to tap twice — a second tap doesn't do anything or create
a duplicate.

**The volunteer checks themselves in by scanning the event QR code.** They enter their email,
see their own shifts as cards with a per-shift verdict (open now, opens at a time, or closed), and
tap the one they're here for. Only that shift is checked in. The QR link carries the event's
four-digit venue code — see the venue codes document for why that matters.

**A mis-tap can be undone.** Tapping a checked-in volunteer on the roster reverts them to
confirmed, and the arrival time is cleared so it can't linger and affect anything downstream. Undo
only works on a checked-in signup: once a session has been closed out and someone is marked
attended or no-show, undo won't move it. The way back from there is **Reopen event**, which undoes
the close-out for the whole event rather than one person — see
[17-ending-a-slot.md](17-ending-a-slot.md).

**Check-in does not grant orientation credit.** Credit is granted when the orientation slot is
*ended*, not when people are tapped in. If an orientation ran and nobody has credit for it, the
usual cause is that the slot was never closed out.

**A volunteer who turns up but never gets tapped in can still be marked attended** while the
organizer closes out the session. Nothing is lost by forgetting to check someone in during the
session itself.

**Organizers can only check in volunteers on their own events.** Check-in, undo, close-out, and
roster reads all verify that the organizer owns the event, so one organizer cannot act on another's
event. Admins can act on any event.
