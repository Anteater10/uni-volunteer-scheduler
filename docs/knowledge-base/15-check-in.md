# Check-in and attendance

**Check-in is per slot, not per event.** A volunteer who is doing three sessions checks in three
times, once for each session they attend. Checking in records that they arrived; it does not by
itself finish anything.

There are two ways a volunteer gets checked in.

**The organizer taps them in on the roster.** This is the normal path. Open the event from
Operations, find the row, tap. A staff tap has no time limit — an organizer can check someone in
before, during, or long after the session, which matters because tidying up attendance the next
morning is a real thing that happens.

**The volunteer checks themselves in by scanning the event QR code.** They enter their email,
see their own shifts as cards with a per-shift verdict (**Open now**, **Opens at** a time, or
**Check-in closed**), and tap the one they're here for. Only that shift is checked in. The QR link
carries the event's four-digit venue code — see the venue codes document for why that matters.

**The 30-minute check-in window applies only to the volunteer's own self-check-in.** A volunteer can
check themselves in from 30 minutes before their slot starts until 30 minutes after it starts —
both ends of the window count from the start time, so on a long session self check-in closes well
before the session ends. Outside that
window the door page tells them when the shift opens or that it has closed, rather than showing a
locked screen with no explanation. The window opens half an hour early because volunteers reliably
arrive early, and it closes because a self-serve link that works forever is a link that gets used
from the car park a week later. None of this restricts staff.

**Tapping a checked-in volunteer a second time undoes the check-in.** The roster tap is a toggle,
not a repeat: a checked-in card says "tap again to undo", and tapping it reverts the volunteer to
confirmed and clears their arrival time so it can't linger and affect anything downstream. This is
the mis-tap fix — you have not lost anything by tapping the wrong row, but do not tap twice
"to be sure", because the second tap takes it back.

**Undo only works on a checked-in signup.** Once a session has been closed out and someone is marked
attended or no-show, that's final from the roster and undo won't move it. The one way back is
reopening the whole event after it is complete — see the document on ending a slot.

**Check-in does not grant orientation credit.** Credit is granted when the orientation slot is
*ended*, not when people are tapped in. If an orientation ran and nobody has credit for it, the
usual cause is that the slot was never closed out.

**A volunteer who turns up but never gets tapped in can still be marked attended** while the
organizer closes out the session — that is the walk-in case, and it works for anyone whose signup
is confirmed. **The exception is a volunteer still sitting at pending**, meaning they never clicked
the confirmation link in their email. Pending volunteers do not appear on the close-out screen at
all, so they cannot be marked attended there. Tap them in on the roster first — the tap confirms
them automatically — because otherwise their unresolved row keeps the session looking open even
after everyone else has been closed out.

**Any organizer or admin can check in volunteers on any event.** Check-in, undo, close-out, and
roster reads are gated on being staff, not on having created the event: the staff event list is
shared, nothing in the app can hand an event over to a different organizer, and organizers are a
trusted role. The boundary that actually exists is the set of admin-only pages — users, audit logs,
quarters, exports, and orientation credits.
