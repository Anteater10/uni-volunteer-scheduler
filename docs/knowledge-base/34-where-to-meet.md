# Where volunteers meet, and how early

This is the answer to "where do I go?" and "how early should I get there?" for both orientation and
module sessions.

**Orientation is in Chem 1005D**, unless the session itself says otherwise. Always check the session
details, because an individual orientation can be posted in a different room.

**For a module session, volunteers meet at Chem 1204, 15 minutes before the module starts.** Chem
1204 is in the Chemistry building at UCSB. To find Chem 1204: enter through the main entrance, walk
straight until you reach the bulletin board, then turn left.

Meeting 15 minutes early at Chem 1204 is not the same as the session start time shown on the signup.
A volunteer reading their confirmation email sees the session time; the meeting time is 15 minutes
before that.

**Transportation to the school is provided by SciTrek.** Volunteers travel from the Chem 1204 meeting
point to the partner school with the group — there is nothing for a volunteer to arrange, book, or
sort out themselves, and no mileage to claim.
