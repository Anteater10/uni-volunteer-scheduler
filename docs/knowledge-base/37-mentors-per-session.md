# How many mentors a session needs, and what "understaffed" means

**A module session running with fewer than 6 mentors is considered understaffed.** Six is the
threshold SciTrek staff use when deciding whether a session needs more volunteers, so a session
sitting at 5 or fewer signups is the one to chase.

"Understaffed" is a staffing judgement, not a status the app enforces. Nothing blocks a session from
running below 6, and the app will not refuse signups or cancellations to protect the threshold. Slot
capacity is a separate, unrelated number: capacity is the maximum a slot will accept before new
signups go to the waitlist, whereas 6 is the minimum staff want to see actually attending.

To find understaffed sessions, use the Operations console — it surfaces upcoming slots with their fill
counts, which is where a slot below 6 becomes visible. Fill rates across a whole quarter come from the
**Event fill rate** report in Admin → Exports.

The usual fixes for an understaffed session are promoting people off the waitlist, or sending a
broadcast to ask for more volunteers.
