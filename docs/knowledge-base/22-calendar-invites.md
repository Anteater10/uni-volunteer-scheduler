# Calendar invites

Volunteers can add a session they've signed up for to their own calendar. The option appears on the
signup success screen and on the confirmation page they reach from their magic link.

There are two ways to do it: **download a calendar file** (an `.ics` file, which works with Apple
Calendar, Outlook, and anything else that reads standard calendar files), or **open a Google
Calendar link** that pre-fills the event.

The calendar entry covers the **slot** the volunteer signed up for — the specific session with its
own date, start and end time, and location. A volunteer taking three sessions adds three entries.

Calendar times are generated with the correct **Pacific timezone** information rather than as bare
local times, so a volunteer whose device is set to another timezone still sees the session at the
right hour.

Calendar invites are **generated on request** from the current state of the signup. There's no
sync: if a session is later moved, an already-added calendar entry does not update itself. Tell
volunteers about changes with a **broadcast**.
