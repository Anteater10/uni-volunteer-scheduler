# Course credit and volunteer hours

SciTrek volunteers can earn either **course credit** or **volunteer hours** for mentoring.

**To claim course credit, a volunteer emails Gulistan Tansik at gulistan@ucsb.edu.** That's the whole
process from the volunteer's side, and it's the answer to "how do I get course credit for this?" The
scheduler does not issue course credit, has no course-credit form, and no button anywhere grants it —
it's handled by email with Gulistan, outside the app.

Note that **course credit is a completely different thing from orientation credit**, despite the
similar name. Orientation credit is the app's internal record that a volunteer has been oriented for a
module family, and it has nothing to do with academic credit.

**Volunteer hours are counted from attendance.** Attendance is recorded when staff close out a
session, which resolves each volunteer to attended or no-show. This is why closing out sessions
matters: a session that was never closed leaves its volunteers sitting at confirmed or checked-in
rather than attended, so their hours don't count. A volunteer asking why their hours look low is
usually asking about a session nobody closed out.

Hours totals per volunteer come from the **Volunteer hours** report in Admin → Exports.
