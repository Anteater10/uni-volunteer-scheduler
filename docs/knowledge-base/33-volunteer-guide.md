# Volunteering with SciTrek — expectations and logistics

This is what a SciTrek volunteer needs to know, and what staff should be able to tell them.

## How to mentor

The heart of the role is **meaningful interaction with students**. Share your enthusiasm for science
and connect with the students you're working with. Be **collaborative, not directive** — lead as a
friend and teammate rather than as an instructor. Stay positive, supportive, and flexible, and
remember what it's like to be 12–14 years old.

Five things SciTrek asks of every mentor:

1. **Build positive relationships** by showing genuine interest, offering praise, and being
   approachable.
2. **Celebrate and recognize** accomplishments and genuine attempts.
3. **Speak with respect** and an expectation of the same. Don't talk down.
4. **Stay supportive, focused, and calm.**
5. **Don't be shy** — share yourself, your interests, background, and feelings — and don't take
   anything personally.

## Where to go

**Orientation is in Chem 1005D**, unless the session says otherwise.

**For a module session, meet at Chem 1204, 15 minutes before the module starts.** Chem 1204 is in the
Chemistry building: enter through the main entrance, walk straight until you reach the bulletin
board, then turn left.

## Getting there

**Transportation to the school is provided by SciTrek.** There's nothing for a volunteer to arrange
or sort out themselves.

## Signing up

Volunteers sign up **session by session**, and may take as many sessions as they like. There's no
account and no password — signing up sends an email with a link for viewing, cancelling, or swapping
later.

**A volunteer who hasn't done orientation for a module must include an orientation session in the
same signup.** This is required, not suggested. The one exception is a module that has no orientation
posted at all, which is rare.

## Cancelling

**Give at least two days' notice** if you can't make a session you signed up for. Cancel through the
link in your signup email. Cancelling promptly matters because it frees the seat for someone on the
waitlist automatically — cancelling late usually means the spot goes unfilled and the classroom is
short a mentor.

## Credit and hours

Volunteers can earn **course credit or volunteer hours**. **To claim course credit, email Gulistan
Tansik at gulistan@ucsb.edu.**

Hours are counted from **attendance**, which staff record by closing out each session — so showing
up and being marked attended is what makes hours count.

## Who to contact

The **SciTrek office** is the contact for everything. For course credit specifically, email
**Gulistan Tansik — gulistan@ucsb.edu**.

## How many mentors a session needs

A module session running with **fewer than 6 mentors is considered understaffed**. That's the
threshold staff use when deciding whether a session needs more volunteers.
